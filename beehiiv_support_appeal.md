# Beehiiv 账号解锁申诉（英文模板）

> 2026-10-20 用户截图确认锁定页面原文：**"Account is locked due to a violation of Terms of Use"**，归类为 **"Fraud, Deception & Sanctioned Entities"**。页面底部有黑色 **Contact support** 按钮，点它联系客服申诉（等同于后台 New chat）。
> 定性判断：账号从未成功发出过一封邮件、第一次发送尝试即被锁——这是新账号自动风控误判的典型形态，归类名是系统分桶，不是人工定罪。申诉时如实说明 + 主动提出愿意做身份核验即可。

下面这段英文可以直接粘贴进客服窗口，把方括号里的内容换成你自己的信息即可。

---

Hello Beehiiv Support,

My account was locked under "Fraud, Deception & Sanctioned Entities" before I could send anything — my very first test send and publish attempt both failed with an "Account is locked" error. I believe this is an automated false positive, and I'm writing to request a manual review.

Account details:
- Email: yangxm4996@gmail.com
- Publication: [你的 publication 名称，例如 SweetAI Weekly]

A few facts about my account:
- I have never sent a single email through Beehiiv, and I have never imported or purchased any subscriber list.
- I am an independent developer publishing "SweetAI Weekly", a curated weekly roundup of AI news and tools. All content is original editorial curation with links to sources, and subscribers join only through the publication's own signup form.
- I operate publicly under my own projects (sweetai.work) and I'm on the Free plan (2,500 subscribers) with no payment method attached — there is no commercial or deceptive activity of any kind.

I'm happy to complete any identity verification you require. Could you please review my account and lift the restriction?

Thank you for your time.

---

## 客服可能追问的答复要点（备用）

- **What type of content will you send?** — A weekly curated newsletter about AI news, AI tools and tech products. Original summaries with links to sources, no scraped or purchased content.
- **How did you collect subscribers?** — Organic signups via the publication website's subscribe form only. No imported lists.
- **Sending frequency / volume?** — Once per week, starting from a very small list (new publication).
- **Are you on the Free plan?** — Yes, Free plan (2,500 subscribers), no payment method attached.
- **Where are you located / who is your audience?** — 如实回答即可（个人开发者，面向对 AI 感兴趣的英文读者）。

## 申诉后

- 客服一般核实后就会解开；如果要求补充材料（可能包括身份核验），按上面要点如实回复即可。
- 解锁前不要反复尝试发送，以免触发更严的限制。
- **不要另注册一个新账号绕开锁定**——那反而违反服务条款，会把误判坐实。
- 解锁后跟我说一声；每天的 newsletter HTML 照常生成，不耽误。
